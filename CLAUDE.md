## Working practice — keep the rolling to-do current

`docs/TODO.md` holds two living lists: **TO DO NOW** (make the software fully
usable for the owner's 1-month live self-test) and **TO DO BEFORE PUBLIC**
(hardening for real customers). Keep it current: after any action or decision
that completes, adds, or re-prioritises an item, update `docs/TODO.md` in the
same commit/PR and reflect the change back to the user in chat. `docs/ROADMAP.md`
is the shipped-history record; `docs/TODO.md` is "what's left, by priority".
